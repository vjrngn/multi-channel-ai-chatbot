# Orders

## Tracking your order
When your order ships we email you a tracking link. You can also find it under My Account › Orders. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show the first scan.

## Changing an order
You can change the size, colour or address of an order within 2 hours of placing it. After that it is packed and cannot be changed, but you can return it once it arrives.

## Cancelling an order
Orders can be cancelled until they ship. Go to My Account › Orders and choose Cancel. If it has already shipped, return it instead.
